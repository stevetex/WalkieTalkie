Privacy policy

Over&Out relays your voice to your friends as you talk and doesn't keep it. We collect only what the app needs to connect you with the friends you choose.

Effective September 28, 2026. Over&Out is made by Cypress Oak Studios LLC.

What we collect

  • Your account. When you sign in with Apple, we receive an identifier Apple creates for Over&Out. We don't store your email address. We keep the screen name you give (or change in Settings), which your friends see and which appears when you ring them.
  • Your picture. Either one of the app's built-in mascots, which we store as a name, or a small square copy of a photo you choose, without its location or other details. Only you and your friends can see it. Change or remove it any time in Settings.
  • Your friends and safety settings. Who you're friends with, which of them you've starred as favorites, when each friend last talked to you (shown on their page in the app), the invites you create (deleted after 7 days or once used), the people you block, and any reports you file.
  • Your devices. A random ID for each iPhone and Apple Watch you sign in on, the push tokens Apple gives us so they can ring (on the iPhone, through Apple's Push to Talk), and which device you want rings on.
  • Diagnostics. How rings and conversations go (when they start, connect and end, whether they were answered, and how long each step took), plus errors, crash and hang reports, and your device's model and its system and app versions, so we can make Over&Out faster and more reliable. These records contain account and device IDs and times, never names, audio or what you say, and are deleted after 30 days.
  • The diagnostics log on your devices. The app keeps a more detailed log of the same kind of events on your iPhone and Apple Watch, for about 14 days. It's sent to us only when you use Report a Problem or, while Over&Out is in TestFlight testing, when we ask your app for it to look into a problem. We delete it after 30 days. In the App Store version, the app will ask you before sending it.
  • Problem reports. What you write in Report a Problem, kept for 90 days.
  • Server logs. Our servers log requests, including IP addresses and account IDs, to keep the service running and secure. Google Cloud deletes them after 30 days.

Your voice

Audio goes from your device through our relay to your friend's device as you talk. If your friend hasn't answered yet, the relay holds your message in memory until they do, for up to about 35 seconds, then drops it. Audio is never written to disk, stored or listened to by us.

So a message plays the moment your friend taps the ring, their Apple Watch may download it while it's ringing. It's kept only on their watch: the app deletes it once it plays, or at the next ring if it never did, and when they sign out.

How we use it

Only to run Over&Out: signing you in, ringing your friends, enforcing blocks, responding to reports, and fixing problems. We don't show ads, track you across apps or websites, or sell or share your data with data brokers.

Who else handles it

  • Google Cloud hosts our servers and database in the United States.
  • Apple provides Sign in with Apple and delivers push notifications.

Reports and blocks

If you report someone, we review the report and may block or delete accounts that break the rules. Reports are kept for safety, with account IDs only, even after either account is deleted.

Deleting your account

In the iPhone app, open Settings and tap Delete Account. We delete your account, screen name, photo, friends, invites, blocks, devices, sessions, diagnostics logs and problem reports, remove you from your friends' lists, and revoke Over&Out's Sign in with Apple access. Diagnostics and server logs expire within 30 days.
