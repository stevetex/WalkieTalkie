A walkie-talkie for iPhone and Apple Watch

Hold the mascot's mouth to talk, and your friend hears you. On a watch they tap the ring to listen; on an iPhone it plays right away. Then you talk back and forth for as long as you like. Only friends you invite can ring you.

  1. Sign in with Apple on your iPhone.
  2. Invite a friend over Messages.
  3. Talk from your watch or your iPhone.
