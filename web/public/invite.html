Over&Out

You've been invited to talk on Over&Out

A walkie-talkie for Apple Watch. Raise your wrist, hold Talk, and your friend hears you.

  1. Get Over&Out on your iPhone and sign in with Apple.
  2. Come back to Messages and tap the invite link again. The app opens and asks whether to add your friend.

Already have the app? Tap the link in Messages rather than opening it in Safari. Invites work once and expire after 7 days.
