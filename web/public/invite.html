You've been invited to talk on Over&Out

A walkie-talkie for iPhone and Apple Watch. Hold the mascot's mouth to talk, and your friend hears you.

  1. Join the Over&Out beta on your iPhone with the button below. It opens TestFlight, Apple's free app for trying apps before they're in the App Store. Install TestFlight if it asks, then install Over&Out from it.
  2. Open Over&Out and sign in with Apple.
  3. Come back to Messages and tap the invite link again. The app opens and asks whether to add your friend.
