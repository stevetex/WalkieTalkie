My watch doesn't ring when Do Not Disturb or a Focus is on

On your iPhone, open Settings → Focus, choose each Focus you use, tap Apps, and add Over&Out to Allow Notifications From. Your watch follows your iPhone.

Messages go to the wrong device

Friends ring one device: your Apple Watch or your iPhone. Choose which in the iPhone app under Settings → Ring Me On. If that device can't be reached, the other one rings.

My iPhone doesn't play messages

In the iPhone app, open Settings and check that Walkie-Talkie is on. Messages then play as soon as they arrive, even with your iPhone locked, and you can reply with the Talk button on the Lock Screen.

How do I add a friend?

In the iPhone app, tap Invite a Friend and send the link in Messages. When your friend taps it, the app asks whether to add you. Each invite works once and expires after 7 days.

How do I block or report someone?

In the iPhone app, tap the friend, then Block or Report. Blocking removes them from your friends, and they can't ring you or send you invites.

How do I delete my account?

In the iPhone app, open Settings and tap Delete Account. See the privacy policy for what's deleted.
